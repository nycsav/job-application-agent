## Best-practice guardrails in place (per Anthropic "Building Effective Agents")
- Human-in-the-loop at the irreversible step (submit). One owner of Notion writes.
- Observability: every write carries an `Agent Trail` (who/when/why).
- Simplicity over theater: no fake "RL", no "100% security" claims. Real controls: PII
  gitignored, secrets as env, read-only email, enforcing hook.
