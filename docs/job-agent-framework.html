THE GATE — you approve before any submit
Between Engine 1 and Engine 2. One-screen review in Notion. This is the rule the whole system is built around.
Scan→ Score + Dedup→ Stage to Notion→ 🔒 You approve→ Apply (Mac)→ File: Email + Notion + Calendar

Why not "apply from here"

This cloud session can read your email and write Notion, but it has no web browser. Submitting a job form needs a logged-in browser — that lives on your Mac.

Why not Notion Agents

Notion Agents automate work inside Notion. They can't drive an outside employer's application form either — so the apply step still belongs to the Mac runner.

Cloud = blue (find & pick) · Local Mac = amber (apply & file) · Black = the human gate. Nothing submits without you.
